# Coca-Cola USA Retailer Sales Dashboard
Interactive Excel dashboard analyzing Coca-Cola USA retail sales data using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI Cards, Slicers, and Timeline Filters.

## Overview

This project analyzes Coca-Cola beverage sales across U.S. retailers, regions, cities, and brands. The workbook includes raw transaction-level sales data, pivot-style analysis, and a dashboard summarizing key business performance metrics.

The goal of this project is to identify sales trends, top-performing brands, regional performance, retailer profitability, and city-level growth opportunities.

## Project Structure

The workbook contains three main sheets:

'Data' - Raw sales transaction data including retailer, date, region, state, city, brand, units sold, total sales, operating profit, and operating margin
'Analyze' - Summary analysis and pivot-style aggregations by brand, region, retailer, quarter, and city
'Dashboard' - Visual dashboard showing sales trends, brand performance, regional mix, operating margin, and top cities

## Key Insights

- The dataset contains 9,648 sales records covering the period from January 2022 to December 2023.
- Total sales were approximately $12.0M, with 24.8M units sold.
- Total operating profit was approximately $4.7M.
- 2023 significantly outperformed 2022, with sales increasing from approximately $2.4M to $9.6M.
- Coca-Cola was the top-performing brand, generating approximately $2.77M in sales.
- Dasani Water was the second-largest brand by sales, generating approximately $2.39M.
- The West region generated the highest sales, while the South region showed stronger profitability characteristics.
- West Soda was the largest retailer by sales.
- FizzyCo had the highest average operating margin among retailers.
- Q3 2023 was the strongest quarter, with approximately $2.81M in sales.
- New York was the top city by operating profit.

## Business Interpretation

The analysis shows strong sales growth in 2023, driven by brand strength, retailer expansion, and regional performance. Coca-Cola and Dasani Water are the strongest contributors to revenue and profit. While the West region leads in sales volume, margin improvement opportunities may exist there compared with more profitable regions such as the South.

Retailer-level analysis suggests that West Soda is important for revenue scale, while FizzyCo is a strong profitability contributor. This creates an opportunity to balance high-volume partnerships with margin-focused retail strategies.
